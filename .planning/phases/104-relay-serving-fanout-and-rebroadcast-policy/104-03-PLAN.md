| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| T-104-03-04 | Denial of Service | local fanout enqueue | mitigate | Use Plan 02 fanout queue caps and do not enqueue duplicate, rejected, orphaned, evicted, or expired outcomes. |
| T-104-03-05 | Elevation of Privilege | local relay fanout | mitigate | Accepted local submissions still pass through the same relay eligibility and suppression policy as peer submissions. |
