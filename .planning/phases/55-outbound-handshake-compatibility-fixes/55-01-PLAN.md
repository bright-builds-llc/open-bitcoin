<objective>
Make daemon sync complete baseline-compatible outbound handshakes with
reachable manual or DNS peers, and record incompatible peers as typed failed
outcomes with no useful progress or durable-state corruption.
</objective>

<threat_model>

## Threat Model

- **Malformed peer data:** Must remain rejected and must not advance accepted
  header or block counters.
- **Wrong-network peer:** Must remain a failed outcome through existing
  `InvalidMagic` handling.
- **Duplicate-version peer:** Must disconnect and produce a failed outcome, not
  silently degrade into a stall or connected peer.
- **False progress claim:** A completed handshake may count as a connected peer,
  but only accepted headers or blocks may update useful progress timestamps or
  durable header/block heights.
- **Public-network nondeterminism:** Default verification must not depend on
  reachable public peers.

</threat_model>
