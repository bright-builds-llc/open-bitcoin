<threat_model>
Threats:
- A renderer-only pressure string can diverge across CLI status, dashboard, RPC, soak, and support.
- Unbounded evidence can serialize raw paths, logs, stores, or large tables into status and support bundles.
- A stop-required classification can be ambiguous and cause hidden mutation or unsafe continuation.

Mitigations:
- Put all labels, levels, units, thresholds, usage values, and next actions in first-party typed structs.
- Keep evidence compact and allowlisted: numeric counts, budgets, byte estimates, labels, and short guidance only.
- Model pressure as `normal`, `warning`, or `stop_required`, with no side effects in the classifier.
- Define `RESOURCE_BOUND_WARNING_PERCENT = 80` and `RESOURCE_BOUND_STOP_PERCENT = 95` in the pure classifier and derive threshold bytes/counts from explicit budgets.
</threat_model>
