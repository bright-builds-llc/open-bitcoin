| datadir soak ledger -> support bundle JSON | Local ledger events may be incomplete, partially written, stale, or contain unsafe strings in typed fields. |
| support bundle JSON -> human/operator sharing | Bundle data may leave the operator machine after manual sharing, so redaction and claim boundaries matter. |
