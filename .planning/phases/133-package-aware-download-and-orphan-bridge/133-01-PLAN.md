| Chainstate shell → pure network evidence | Active-tip authority and fresh tweak cross into deterministic state |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T-133-01 | Denial of Service | `RollingGenerationFilter` | mitigate | Preallocate fixed words from 120000/0.000001, rotate three generations, assert unchanged allocation under one million inserts |
| T-133-02 | Spoofing | hard/reconsiderable key APIs | mitigate | Accept only typed wtxid/package-digest keys; remove raw txid and byte insertion |

| T-133-05 | Information Disclosure | filter tweak | accept | Tweak is ephemeral, not persisted or exposed; membership is not operator evidence in this phase |
