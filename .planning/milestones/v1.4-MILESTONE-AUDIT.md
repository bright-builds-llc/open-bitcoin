## Integration Check

The `gsd-integration-checker` result was:

- Integration score: 22/22
- Flow score: 5/5
- Conclusion: tech_debt
- Blockers: none

Verified E2E flows:

1. Handshake diagnostics are covered by `packages/open-bitcoin-network/src/compatibility.rs` and deterministic compatibility tests.
2. Handshake outcomes flow into header progress through `DurableSyncRuntime`, peer handshake state, `SyncStopReason`, target header bounds, and durable status projection.
3. Header progress flows into bounded block download/connect through `sync/block_reconcile.rs`, `sync/block_response.rs`, `network.rs`, and live-smoke `firstBlockProgress`.
4. Restart/resume evidence is wired through `scripts/run-live-mainnet-smoke.ts` with `--restart-after-progress`, same-datadir checks, duplicate-connect verdicts, and recovery diagnosis.
5. Operator evidence and release boundaries are wired through status, dashboard, RPC, support evidence, `OpenBitcoinStatusSnapshot`, `scripts/check-v1.4-release-boundaries.ts`, and deterministic `scripts/verify.sh` exclusion of public-network live-smoke commands.
