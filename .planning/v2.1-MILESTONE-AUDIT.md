## Non-Critical Tech Debt

### Phase 112 / Phase 118

- Inbound `GetBlockTxn` remains a no-op in `peer/message_dispatch.rs`. The current requirements cover outbound missing requests and inbound responses, but not serving missing transactions to a remote peer after a locally originated compact announcement.
- Rename the stale `phase112_bip152_wire_messages_are_peer_noops` test so it describes the Phase 112 boundary without contradicting later live shell routing.

### Phase 116

- `BlockServedCount` is derived from `eligible_peer_count`, not a distinct successful block-wire-emission counter.

### Phase 120

- Timeout expiry is receive-driven. A completely idle connection will not expire until another receive advances the supplied timestamp.

### Phase 121

- The daemon provider samples the shared `ManagedRpcContext`, which is separate from `DurableSyncRuntime::network`; sync-runtime compact activity may not appear in the projected series.

### Milestone Closeout

- `ROADMAP.md` and the `REQUIREMENTS.md` coverage footer still say seven gaps are pending even though the requirement checklist, traceability table, summaries, phase verifications, and this audit show all seven closed.

## Audit Conclusion

The v2.1 definition of done is met and archival is not blocked. The remaining items are non-critical debt. Before invoking milestone completion, choose whether to accept them into future backlog or create a focused cleanup phase.
