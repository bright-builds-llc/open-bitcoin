## Critical Integration Gaps

### GAP-01 — Durable sync does not feed inbound serving or RPC status

`open-bitcoind` constructs the RPC/inbound network at
`packages/open-bitcoin-rpc/src/bin/open-bitcoind.rs:68-75` and the durable sync
runtime separately at lines 351-399. The RPC context creates a fresh
`MemoryChainstateStore` in
`packages/open-bitcoin-rpc/src/context/network.rs:95-102`; its durable recovery
restores only mempool state at lines 430-465. Durable sync restores chainstate
into its own network in `packages/open-bitcoin-node/src/sync.rs:90-107`.

### GAP-02 — No production compact negotiation/announcement emitter

Inbound `sendcmpct` handling exists, but the handshake at
`packages/open-bitcoin-network/src/peer/message_dispatch.rs:156-173` sends no
local `sendcmpct`. `ManagedPeerNetwork::announce_block` has no production
caller or transport write, and
`packages/open-bitcoin-node/src/network.rs:401-409` substitutes constant peer
header facts.

### GAP-03 — Compact-announcement evidence precedes transport success

`packages/open-bitcoin-node/src/network.rs:428-451` records announcement state
when it returns a `CompactBlock` message. Unlike the full-block path, no
successful write acknowledgement gates the evidence.
