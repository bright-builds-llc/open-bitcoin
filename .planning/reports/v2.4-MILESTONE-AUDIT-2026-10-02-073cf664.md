## Blocking integration findings

### INT-01: automatic retention has no production caller

**Requirements:** PRUN-01 partial, PRUN-02 unsatisfied. **Secondary impact:** OPER-01 displays configured automatic mode, and LOCK-01 has no automatic execution consumer.

Configure `prune: 550`, retain more than the target with eligible historical payloads, and let ordinary periodic/shutdown flushing run. Configuration, limited-service advertisement, and automatic-target status work, but the target never causes deletion.

`AutomaticPruneInput` and `plan_automatic_prune` exist at `packages/open-bitcoin-chainstate/src/prune/plan.rs:24` and `:40`. Their Rust references are exports and unit tests. The daemon calls `handle.flush_coins` at `packages/open-bitcoin-rpc/src/bin/open_bitcoind/coins_flush.rs:159`; `packages/open-bitcoin-node/src/network/runtime_authority.rs:539` delegates to flush and supplies `PrunePlan::default()` at `:544`. The manual wrapper documents that empty ordinary-flush path at `packages/open-bitcoin-node/src/network/runtime_authority/prune_flush.rs:45`.

**Closure needed:** obtain authoritative per-height usage, target, chain parameters, and current durable locks in the shell; call the existing pure automatic planner from the running flush lifecycle; apply its plan through the same durable owner and preserve cache/error semantics. Add an integration test that configures automatic mode, exceeds the target, triggers the production lifecycle, observes paired deletion/have-pruned, and checks keep-window, prune-after, and lock protection. A pure planner test alone cannot close this gap.

### INT-02: rescan admits entries without checking earlier creating payloads

**Requirement:** SNAP-01 partial against Phase 146 locked D-04. Leftover snapshot bytes are correctly non-authoritative.

Prune a creating height `h`, retain an unspent matching coin created there, then rescan from `h + 1` through a retained tip. The requested range can pass every presence check, but the full wallet rebuild still admits the older coin without probing its creating payload.

`.planning/phases/146-wallet-leftover-snapshot-cutover/146-CONTEXT.md:49` requires a creating payload for every admitted wallet UTXO/history entry. D-08's chunk gate does not waive D-04. Node rescan gates only its current chunk at `packages/open-bitcoin-node/src/sync/wallet_rescan.rs:121`, then includes all coins created through the chunk end at `:183`. RPC gates only the requested range at `packages/open-bitcoin-rpc/src/context/rescan.rs:165`, then invokes rebuilding at `:188`. `packages/open-bitcoin-wallet/src/wallet/scan.rs:26` copies matching snapshot coins and `:52` replaces the entire wallet coin set.

This finding does not claim durable coins are invalid or balances numerically wrong. It demonstrates missing agreed payload eligibility. A scan starting inside the deleted range correctly fails closed.

**Closure needed:** enforce creating-height payload eligibility for every entry supplied to a full replacement, or use a well-defined incremental scan that admits only verified entries. Cover both node chunk/resume and RPC midrange scans after a real paired prune. Preserve the no-leftover-fallback contract and durable job failure evidence.
